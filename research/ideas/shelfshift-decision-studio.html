Model assumptions and boundaries

Remaining quality hours = max(0, nominal remaining hours − (existing warm exposure + extra exposure) × 1.5). The 1.5 coefficient is arbitrary for demonstration; it is not a calibrated biological model. Baseline expected sales = min(stock, daily sales × demand multiplier × remaining hours / 24). Unsold share = remaining unsold units / stock.

Rotation assumes a 10% demand uplift; markdown assumes a 35% uplift and a 15% price cut. Recovery removes at most the confirmed capacity from baseline surplus, and is available only when the window exceeds the demo six-hour collection lead time. All uplift, collection and freshness assumptions need pilot validation. Estimates can be fractional. Revenue excludes costs; recovery earns no sales revenue. No emissions calculation is claimed.

Blocked or unreviewed batches cannot be approved. A demo review is not a real safety assessment. The prototype does not change printed dates or validate food suitability. Each approval replaces the earlier proposal for that batch; no cumulative savings are claimed.
