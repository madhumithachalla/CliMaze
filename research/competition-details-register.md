# **Verified competition update**

Official event details now confirmed:

\- Climate Hack-tion is funded by the European Union and delivered online through the Junction platform.

\- Eligible participants are university or TAFE students aged 18+ from Australia, New Zealand, the listed Pacific Islands and EU member states. EU students may join eligible regional teams; EU-only teams are not permitted.

\- Teams must contain 3–5 people. CliMaze currently has five members and meets this size requirement.

\- The 60-hour online hackathon runs from 9:00 am AEST on 2 October 2026 to 9:00 pm AEDT on 4 October 2026\. The Junction page lists registration closing at 9:00 pm on 2 October 2026 (UTC+10).

\- Teams must produce a working prototype or realistic mock-up addressing one priority: electrification; zero waste and methane reduction; resilient cities and buildings; green industrialisation; or climate awareness.

\- Total prizes exceed A\$10,000: A\$6,000 main prize, A\$3,500 runner-up and a People’s Choice product valued at A\$1,500. Winners are announced on 12 October 2026\.

\- Organiser email action: every member must update their personal registration with the exact registered team name “CliMaze” and the team-member details so the platform can associate the registrations correctly.

\- COP31 context: Türkiye hosts COP31 in Antalya from 9–20 November 2026; Australia is President of Negotiations; Fiji hosts the Pre-COP and Tuvalu a Leaders’ event from 5–8 October 2026\.
