# Demo walkthrough

Press **▶ Guided demo** in the top bar for the same six steps inside the app. Reset to synthetic records under Project & backup first if you have changed anything (export first).

## Two-minute pitch script

| Time | Screen | Say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Overview | "Food businesses rotate stock by the date on the label. These two strawberry batches have the **same** date. One sat in a 22 °C loading bay." |
| 0:15 | Overview · Climate lens | "Rotating by receipt order leaves **24 kg** at risk of waste tomorrow. Freshness-aware planning gets that to **0–4 kg**: about **50 kg CO₂-e** of landfill methane avoided, using the Australian NGA landfill factor. Modelled, and labelled as modelled." |
| 0:35 | Expiry Twin (ST-B) | Show the steeper curve. Drag the what-if slider from 4 °C to 12 °C: "Every degree in storage changes the window. This is Abhishek's Expiry Twin." |
| 0:55 | Demand forecast | "Madhumitha's planning: a seven-day baseline, or 100 covers × 0.2 kg ÷ 80 % yield = 25 kg for hospitality and production." |
| 1:10 | Stock planner | Strawberries, retail, today 10 kg, tomorrow 30 kg, need-by 6 h: uses 10 kg of ST-B first, carries 24 kg of ST-A, orders only 6 kg, and flags 14 kg for earlier action. |
| 1:25 | Overview · Rescue ladder → Actions | Click **Prefill proposal**: "The food recovery hierarchy picks the next best action: prevent, feed people, check, recover." Create the proposal; approve it with a reviewer name. |
| 1:45 | Outcomes & evidence | Record 4 kg with `Demo receipt 001`. "Recorded evidence and modelled impact are never mixed. That's what makes the climate number trustworthy." |
| 2:00 | Close | "Prevent waste before it exists. Every batch, every day." |

## Detailed checks

1. Expiry Twin: ST-B's warmer history gives about 27.3 h of estimated quality; ST-A is capped at 48 h by its date limit.
2. Actions: ordinary rotation 24 kg at risk; earliest-quality-first 4 kg (≈42 kg CO₂-e less); markdown and transfer 0 kg (highlighted as lowest waste risk). Markdown depends on the 40 % uplift assumption.
3. Hospitality: recipe calculator sets today's requirement; the stock planner consumes it.
4. Export the outcome CSV and full workspace JSON. Reload: saved local records remain. Restore the JSON to move the workspace to another browser.
