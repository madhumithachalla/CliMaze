1. Open Project & backup and reset to synthetic records if needed. Export anything important first.
2. Overview names the combined concept: Madhumitha's food-business planning plus Abhishek's Expiry Twin.
3. Open Expiry Twin. ST-A and ST-B share a printed date limit; ST-B's warmer history gives it about 27.3 hours of estimated quality remaining, while ST-A is capped at 48 hours.
4. Open Stock planner. Set strawberries, retail, today 10 kg, tomorrow 30 kg and need-by 6 hours. Recalculate.
5. The plan uses 10 kg from ST-B first, carries 24 kg from ST-A, requests 6 kg replenishment and flags 14 kg for earlier action. Quantity alone would hide the problem.
6. Switch to hospitality and use the recipe calculator: 100 covers × 0.2 kg / 80% yield = 25 kg ingredient need. The stock planner consumes that new requirement. Production uses the same ingredient conversion with output units.
7. Open Actions & approvals. Review scenario assumptions; they are not verified outcomes. Propose a 5 kg sale/use against ST-B with a note. Enter a reviewer and confirm checks to approve.
8. Open Outcomes & evidence. Record 3 kg with a reference such as `Demo receipt 001`. Stock decreases by 3 kg; the partial approval closes. No prevented-emissions claim appears.
9. Export the outcome CSV and full workspace JSON. Reload: saved local records remain. Restore the JSON to move the workspace to another browser.
